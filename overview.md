## What Makes Brits PvE Worlds Unique?

Brits PvE Worlds offers a heavily customized Rust experience featuring:

- PvE-focused gameplay with optional PvP areas
- RPG-style progression and Prestige system
- Hundreds of custom items and mechanics
- Custom bosses and world events
- A player-driven economy
- Long-term progression across wipes
- Frequent updates and new content

---

> 💡 **Wipe Schedule**
>
> There are two kinds of wipe:
>
> - **Map + RP wipe:** roughly every two weeks, always on a **Thursday at 19:00 UK time**. Bases, the map and RP are reset; your levels, skills and prestige are kept. **Next:** Thursday, 1 October 2026 (19:00 UK time = 18:00 UTC).
> - **XP wipe:** about every 6 months. Levels reset (see **[Progression & Levels](/wiki/progression-levels)**). **Next:** Thursday, 4 March 2027 (19:00 UK time = 19:00 UTC).
>
> UK time is BST (UTC+1) in summer and GMT (UTC+0) in winter, which is why the UTC time shifts.
>
> Exact dates and times are posted in the **wipe-feed** channel on Discord. If the dates above look old, that channel is the source of truth.
