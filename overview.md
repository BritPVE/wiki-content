# Welcome to Brits PvE Worlds

Welcome to the official community guide for **Brits PvE Worlds**.

Whether you're joining the server for the first time or you're a long-time veteran, this wiki is designed to help you understand the server's many custom systems, progression paths, events, and unique features.

You'll find step-by-step guides, tips, and reference pages covering everything from getting started to end-game content.

---

## What You'll Find

This wiki covers topics such as:

- Getting started and beginner progression
- Skills and leveling
- Quests and progression paths
- RP farming methods
- Virtual Quarries
- Food & Cooking
- Combat guides (Helis, Bradleys, Raids & Dungeons)
- Legendary weapons, tools and armor
- Prestige perks
- Commands and useful server features

---

## What Makes Brits PvE Worlds Unique?

Brits PvE Worlds offers a heavily customized Rust experience featuring:

- PvE-focused gameplay with optional PvP areas
- RPG-style progression and Prestige system
- Hundreds of custom items and mechanics
- Custom bosses and world events
- A player-driven economy
- Long-term progression across wipes
- Frequent updates and new content
