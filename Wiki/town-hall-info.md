# Town Halls

Welcome to the **Town Halls** section of the Brits PvE Worlds Wiki.

This category serves as central archive for developer Town Hall meetings, where the development team answers community questions, discusses future plans, and shares progress on upcoming features.

Town Halls are intended to keep the community informed about the direction of the server and provide insight into both current and future development.

---

## What can I find here?

This category contains several pages designed to help players quickly find the information they're looking for.

### Q&A

A simplified collection of the most important questions asked by the community and the answers provided by developers.

Questions and answers are rewritten for clarity while preserving the original meaning.

---

### Upcoming Changes

A summary of features, improvements, balance changes, and quality of life updates that have been confirmed or are actively being worked on.

Whenever possible, features are grouped by their current development status:

- **Confirmed** - Features that have been confirmed by the development team.
- **In Development** - Features that are currently being worked on.
- **Under Consideration** - Ideas or suggestions that developers have expressed interest in but have not committed to implementing yet.

---

## Important 

Town Hall discussions reflect the current plans of the development team at the time they were held.

As development progresses, priorities may change, features may be delayed, reworked, or cancelled entirely.

Unless otherwise stated, information in this category should be considered **informational rather than guaranteed**.

---

## Want to contribute?

If you notice missing information, outdated content, or would like to help summarize future Town Halls, contrubutions are always welcome.

Together we can help keep the community informed and ensure everyone has easy access to the latest information shared by the development team.
