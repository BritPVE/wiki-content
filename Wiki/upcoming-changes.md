# Upcoming Changes

This page contains a summary of features, improvements, balance changes, and quality of life updated that have been discussed by the development team during Town Halls.

Features listed here are grouped by their current development status.

> [!NOTE]
> Features listed on this page are based on information shared by the developers during Town Halls. Development plans may change, and features may be delayed, modified, or cancelled before release.

---

# 🚧 In Development

These features have been confirmed and are actively being worked on.

## Proxy V2

A complete rewrite of the server proxy designed to improve stability, performance, scalability, and support future systems such as map switching.

---

## Launch Site Raid

A brand-new raid experience currently in the late stages of development and expected to be one of the flagship features of V2.

---

## Player-Owned Shops

Players will be able to create and manage their own shops within the Hub.

The system is also expected to support direct player trading and world switching.

---

## Wiki Integration

The current `/info` menu is planned to be replaced with a system that pulls information directly from the community wiki, allowing guides and information to stay up to date more easily.

---

# 📋 Planned

These features have been confirmed but are still in earlier stages of development.

## Custom Skill Tree

The existing Skill Tree system is planned to be replaced with a completely custom implementation built specifically for Brits PvE Worlds.

---

## Map Switching

Future updates aim to introduce multiple unique maps instead of using the same map across every world.

This will allow entirely new areas and progression tiers to be added.

---

## Tier 2 Progression

Following V2, developers plan to expand progression with Tier 2 content, including Deep Sea and the new Launch Site Raid.

Future progression will eventually expand into Tier 3 content with additional custom worlds.

---

## Loot Table Rebalance

Developers plan to rebalance legendary drop locations, event rewards, and general loot progression to encourage a wider variety of gameplay.

---

## Economy Rework

The economy will continue to receive balancing adjustments, including marketplace improvements, taxes, pricing, and reward distribution.

---

# 💡 Under Consideration

The following ideas have been discussed by the developers but have **not** been confirmed.

- Chicken Coop automation improvements
- Offline Sacrifice Board rewards
- Everlasting candles
- Better monument loot
- Additional community events
- Improved contextual tutorials for new players
- Expanded player-to-player marketplace
- Larger building limits
