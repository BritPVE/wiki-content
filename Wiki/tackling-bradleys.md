# Tackling Bradleys

Bradleys are one of the easiest repeatable PvE activities on Brits PvE Worlds, making them an excellent source of loot for both new and experienced players.

## Quick Overview

- **Recommended For:** Early to Mid Game
- **Difficulty:** ⭐⭐☆☆☆
- **Main Goal:** Destroy bradleys efficiently while minimilizing damage taken.

---

## Recommended Gear

Unlike Patrol Helis, Bradleys don't require expensive equipment. Good position is far more important than your gear.

### Recommended Weapons

- Rocket Launcher
- **Ashmaker** *(Highly Recommended)*
- **Huff-N-Puff** *(Highly Recommended)*

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Ashmaker and Huff-N-Puff destroy Bradleys much faster than a standard Rocket Launcher, but even basic launchers are perfectly viable.

---

## Starting the Event

1. Use **`/hub`**.
2. Enter one of the red portals leading to **Raid World 1-4**.
3. Travel to one of the Bradley Islands.
4. Open the store using **`/s`**.
5. Navigate to the **Buyable Bradleys** section and purchase one.

---

## Combat Strategy

Once purchased, the Bradley will be dropped from the sky.

You **cannot damage it while it's airbrone**, so wait until it lands before attacking.

If you're using a **Huff-N-Puff**, you can begin firing just before it lands to maximize your damage.

During the fight:

- Stay behind the side walls for cover.
- Peek only when firing.
- Avoid remaining exposed for too long, as Bradley rockets deal significant damage.

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Good positioning is much more important than expensive gear when fighting Bradleys.

---

## Loot Rules

After destroying the Bradley, several loot crates will spawn in the middle of the arena.

- ✅ Only **you** can open your crates.
- ✅ Other players **cannot** open your crates.
- ⚠ Once opened, the crates disappear and drop all loot onto the ground.

> 💡 **Loot Tip**
>
> Quickly collect the items you want before the despawn or become difficult to manage.

---

## Cooldowns

Bradley cooldowns are tracked separately for each Raid World.

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> You can switch between **Raid Worlds 1–4** to continue farming without waiting for a cooldown to expire.
