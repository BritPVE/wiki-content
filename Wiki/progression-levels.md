# Progression & Levels

Learn how the levelling system works, the fastest ways to gain XP, and how to prepare for Prestige.

## Quick Overview

- **Difficulty:** ⭐⭐☆☆☆
- **Recommended For:** All Players.
- **Main Goal:** Reach higher levels efficiently and prepare for Prestige.

---

## Overview

Progression is one of the core mechanics of Brits PvE Worlds. Every level grants **2 Skill Points**, which can be invested into the **Skill Tree (`/st`)** to unlock powerful upgrades for gathering, combat, raiding and much more.

---

## Level Resets & Prestige

Levels reset **every 6 months** to keep progression fresh and to accomodate new updates and balance changes.

Every **100 levels**, you can **Prestige (`/pr`)**, earning exclusive rewards. The maximum Prestige level is **Pr10**.

---

## Fastest Way to Level Up

The fastest and most consistent way to gain XP is by completing **Quartermaster Tasks** up to **Tier 4**.

To maximize efficiency, use the appropriate legendary tools and resource sets for each task.

---

## Quartermaster Tasks

Quartermaster Tasks are the fastest and most consistent way to gain XP. The right equipment can drastically reduce the time needed to complete them.

### Wood Quartermaster

**Recommended Equipment**
- **Brits Broken Axe** *(optional, even if most players prefer not to use it)* - Has a chance to spawn four ore nodes when chopping trees, making it useful if you're completing multiple Quartermaster tasks.
- **Full Lumberjack Set** - Increases wood yield by **100%**.
  
This setup is the fastest way to complete wood gathering tasks.

---

### Stone, Metal & Sulfur Quartermaster

These three Quartermaster tasks all benefit from the same setup.

**Recommended Equipment**
- **Brits Broken PIckaxe** *(required)* - Increases node yield by **60%**.
- **Full Mining  Set** - Increases mining yield by **100%**.

Together, these provide a combined **160% mining yield bonus**, making them the best setup for mining Quartermaster tasks.

---
