# Item Bank & Auto-Sell

The **Item Bank** and **Auto-Sell** perks allow you to generate passive RP by automatically selling items on the Stock Market even while you're offline.

## Quick Overview

- **Requirement:** Unlock the **Item Bank** Prestige perk.
- **REcommended For:** PLayers looking to earn passive RP.
- **Main Goal:** Automatically sell items stored in your Item Bank.

---

## How It Works

Once you've deposited items into your Item Bank, Auto-Sell will continuously list them on the Stock Market using the price you've selected.

As long as your bank still contains stock, your items will continue selling automatically.

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Auto-Sell stops automatically once your Item Bank runs out of stock, so remember to refill it.

---

## Setup Guide

### Step 1 - Open the Item Bank

Open the Shop using **`/s`**, navigate to the **Stock Market** tab, then click **BANK** on the left-hand side.

![Bank tab in Stock Market](../assets/auto_sell/bank-tab.png)

---

### Step 2 - Deposit Your Items

Deposit the items you want to sell automatically.

These items become your available stock for Auto-Sell.

---

### Step 3 - Locate Your Items

Return to the **Stock Market** and search for the item you just deposited.

---

### Step 4 - Set Your Selling Price

Open the item's **Stock Item Deatils**, enter your desired **Auto-Sell Price**, then confirm. 

![Auto-sell price field](../assets/auto_sell/auto-sell.png)

---

### Step 5 - Confirmation

If everything was configured correctly, you'll receive a confirmation popup.

![Confirmation popup](../assets/auto_sell/confirmation.png)

--- 

## Tips

| Tip | Explanation |

| 📈 Check Price History | Use the market graph to set competitive prices. |
| ⭐ Favourite Items | Star frequently sold items for faster access. |
