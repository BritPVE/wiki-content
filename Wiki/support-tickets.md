# Support Tickets

Need help with a bug, missing item, or another server-related issue? This guide explains when to contact the staff team and how to open a support ticket.

## Quick Overview

- **Recommended For:** All Players.
- **Main Goal:** Contact staff quickly and provide enough information to resolve your issue.

---

## Before Opening a Ticket

Before contacting staff, make sure you have as much information as possible about your issue.

If applicable, include:

- Screenshots
- Video Clips
- Timestamps
- Names of players involved
- A detailed explanation of what happened

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> The more information you provide, the faster staff can investigate your issue.

---

## Recording Clips

Some situations require video evidence, especially when reporting bugs, lost items, or players breaking server rules.

If possible always record short clips while playing.

You can follow the guide available on the **Discord** to set up Steam's build-in game recording feature.

---

## How to Open a Ticket

1. Join the **Discord** server.
2. Go to the **Create-a-Ticket** channel.
3. Select the category that best matches your issue.
4. The ticket bot will send you a Direct Message (DM).
5. Follow the instructions provided by the bot.

### Not Receiving the DM?

Your Discord privacy settings may be blocking it.

To enable Direct Messages:

1. Right-click the server icon.
2. Select **Privacy Settings**.
3. Enable **Allow Direct Messages from Server Members**.

Once enabled, repreat the ticket creation process.

---

## Ticket Categories

### RP Refund

Open this ticket if a bug or server issue caused you to lose RP.

Examples include:

- Raid Bases
- Bosses
- Store purchases
- Other RP-related losses
  
Be as detailed as possible to help the developers identify and fix the issue.

---

### Lost Items

For items lost due to bugs or server issues.

Example:

- Yellow Backpack item loss

---

### General Support

Use this category for:

- Server questions
- Gameplay help
- Technical assistance

---

### Player Report

Open this ticket if another player has broken the server rules.

Whenever possible, include screenshots or video evidence.

---

### VIP Support

Use this category for:

- VIP activation
- Missing VIP rewards
- VIP-related questions

---

### Ban or Mute appeal

Use this ticket to appeal bans or mutes.

> ⚠ **Important**
>
> Do **not** discuss bans or mutes in public chat. Always use a support ticket.

---

### Staff Applications

Interested in helping the community?

Use this category to apply to become a member of the staff team.

---

## Response Times

Support tickets are handled by voluntter staff members.

Typical response times range from **1-5 minutes**, but depending on staff availability, they may take several hours.

Please be patient, someone will respond as soon as they're available.
