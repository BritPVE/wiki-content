# Feedback and Ideas

Have an idea for the server or feedback about existing content? The best place to share it is the **#suggestions** chanle on Discord.

Staff members regularly read suggestions, even if they do not reply directly.

> ⚠ **Important**
>
> Please do **not** tag staff members in suggestions. They will see your post when they are available.

---

## How to Write a Good Suggestion

A Good suggestion is clear, constructive, and easy for others to understand.

Before posting:

1. **Think through your idea**
   - Avoid posting the first thought that comes to mind.
   - Try to explain what problem your idea solves or what it improves.

2. **Search for similar suggestions**
   - Use Discord's search function before creating a new post.
   - If a similar suggestion already exists, add your thoughts there instead of making a duplicate one.
  
3. **Be constructive**
   - Explain the issue clearly.
   - Offer possible solutions when you can.
   - Describe how your idea would improve the server.

4. **Give useful feedback**
   - If you disagree with another suggestion, explain why.
   - Avoid replies like "bad idea" without reasoning.
   - Suggest alternatives if you have a better approach.

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> The best suggestions explain both the **problem** and the **solution**.
