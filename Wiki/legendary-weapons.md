# Legendary Weapons

Legendary Weapons are custom weapons unique to Brits PvE Worlds. Each weapon has a specialized role, making it more effective in certain situations such as fighting Patrol Helis, Bradleys, Scientists, or Raids.

Names below are the in-game item names. Where players usually call a weapon something else, the nickname is in brackets.
