# Legendary Tools

Legendary Tools provide unique abilities that greatly improve gathering, farming, and progression.

## Quick Overview

- **Recommended For:** All Players
- **Main Goal:** Increase gathering efficiency and speed up progression.

| Tool | Best For |
|------|----------|
| Brits Broken Axe | Wood Farming & Quartermaster |
| Brits Broken Pickaxe | Mining & Quartermaster |
| Fertile Bone | Crop Farming |
| Reaping Spade | Large Farms |
| Bal0o's Marrowpaw | Bone Farming |
| Mad's Magical Tools | Repairing items |
| Mad's Extra Magical Tools | Fully repairing items |

Drop sources below come from the wiki's **[Item Drops](/loot-tables)** page, which is generated from the server's loot tables. Exact drop chances are not published by the developers.

## Brits Broken Axe

![Brits Broken Axe](/api/lookup/skins/2837145450/image)

A legendary axe designed to improve resource gathering.

**Best For**
- Wood Quartermaster Tasks
- General wood farming

**Ability:** Has a chance to spawn **4 trees** around you whenever you chop down a tree.

**Drop sources:** Patrol Helis (Easy, Medium, Multi Easy, Multi Medium); Bradley locked crates (Easy); Raidable Bases (Easy, Medium); Dungeons (Normal, Medium, Hard, Nightmare); Deep Sea cargo (military crates); Dome Event (elite crate, heli crate); Sat Dish Event (elite crate); world crates (APC, Canon, elite, elite underwater, locked, Ghost Ship locked, Oil Rig locked, heli, present drop, supply drop).

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Pair it with the **Lumberjack Set** to maximize your wood farming efficiency.

---

## Brits Broken Pickaxe

![Brits Broken Pickaxe](/api/lookup/skins/863332952/image)

A legendary pickaxe focused on maximizing ore production.

**Best For**
- Stone, Metal and Sulfur
- Quartermaster Tasks

**Ability:** Increases ore yield by **60%**.

**Drop sources:** Patrol Helis (Easy, Medium, Multi Easy, Multi Medium); Bradley locked crates (Easy); Raidable Bases (Easy, Medium); Dungeons (Easy, Normal, Medium); Deep Sea cargo (military crates); Dome Event (elite crate, heli crate); Sat Dish Event (elite crate); world crates (APC, Canon, elite, elite underwater, locked, Ghost Ship locked, Oil Rig locked, heli, present drop, supply drop).

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Combine it with the **Mining Set** for a total **160% mining yield bonus**.

---

## Fertile Bone

![Fertile Bone](/api/lookup/skins/2994060062/image)

One of the most important farming tools for players interested in making RP.

**Best For**
- Flower / Hemp farming
- Large crop farms

**Ability:** Consumes **50 Bone Fragments per crop growth stage** to instantly advance crop growth.

**Drop sources:** Wild collectible plants only (every berry bush, hemp, corn, potato, pumpkin, mushroom, wheat, orchid, rose and sunflower).

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Pick every wild plant you walk past: wild plants are the only loot source for Fertile Bones (other than buying them from players).

---

## Reaping Spade

![Reaping Spade](/api/lookup/items/icon/-1536855921)

A farming tool that dramatically speeds up harvesting.

**Best For**
- Flower / Hemp farming
- Large-scale farming

**Ability:** Harvests every plant within a **6-metre radius** (approximately a **3x3 planter setup**) with a single use.

**Drop sources:** World collectibles (the small stone, metal, sulfur and wood pickups); Cobalt Assault (legendary crate); Large Presents.

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Use it on the **center planter** of your 3×3 farm for maximum efficiency.

---

## Bal0o's Marrowpaw

![Bal0o's Marrowpaw](/api/lookup/skins/1703079727/image)

A legendary harvesting tool designed specifically for farming Bone Fragments.

**Best For**
- Bone Fragment farming

**Ability:** Increases Bone Fragment yield from harvested animals by **4x**.

**Drop sources:** Bear corpses; Large Presents.

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> Pair it with the **Hunter Set**, **Survival's/Bear Pie**, and **Super Serum** for the highest possible Bone Fragment yield.

---

## Mad's Magical Tools

A set of tools you can **drag** onto a damaged item to repair it. The item is **single use** and stacks up to 50.

---

## Mad's Extra Magical Tools

A significantly rarer set of tools that **fully repairs** any item it is used on. The item is **single use** and stacks up to 50.
