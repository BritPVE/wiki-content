# Town Hall Q&As

This page contains simplified answers to the most common questions asked during developer Town Halls.

Questions and answers are rewritten for clarity while preserving the original meaning of the original meaning of the developers' responses.

---

## General

### Can I ask questions during Town Halls?

Yes. Before each Town Hall, a suggestion thread is opened on Discord where players can submit questions. If time allows, additional questions may also be answered live during the event.

### Are Town Hall announcements guaranteed?

No. Information shared during Town Halls reflects the developers' current plans and may change during development.

---

## Server & Development

### Why is V2 taking so long?

The developers explained that V2 is much more than content update. It includes a complete rewrite of the server proxy, support for map switching, economy changes, and several large gameplay features. Because of its size, much of the team's development time is currently focused on V2.

### Why aren't more small updates being released?

As V2 approaches, the team has shifted most development resources toward completing the large update instead of releasing smaller pieces individually.

---

## Gameplay

### Can dungeon quests be skipped?

No. 

The current quest progression is intentional and is designed around future Tier 2 and Tier 3 content. Developers want players to progress through the different stages instead of skipping earlier content. 

### Will PVP be added to Worlds?

Not to the current PvE servers.

The developers have confirmed that worlds will remain a PvE experience. However, they are interested in creating more PvP focussed events, such as the Arena.

### Why aren't more Codefling plugins added? 

The developers no longer want to rely on third-party plugins for major gameplay features.

Instead, they prefer developing their own systems that are optimized specifically for Brits PvE Worlds and easier to maintain.

### Why aren't virtual versions of every system being added?

The goal is to keep Rust feeling like Rust.

While automation and quality-of-life features are welcome, the developer want players to continue interaction with physical systems rather than replacing everything with command-based menus.

---

## Economy

### Will the economy continue to change? 

Yes.

The developers regularly monitor player behavior and economic data between wipes. Prices, loot tables, taxes, and rewards will continue to be adjusted over time, especially with the V2 update.

### Why wasn't the economy rolled back after recent issues?

The developers explained that rolling back progress would negatively impact more players than simply correcting the issue for future wipes, especially on a PvE server.

## Information & Wiki

### Is  `/info` being replaced?

Yes.

The current `/info` menu is planned to be replaced by a new system that pulls information directly from the official community wiki (this), making it much easier to keep information up to date and maintain.
