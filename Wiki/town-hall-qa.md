# Town Hall Q&A

This page summarizes questions and answers discussed during recent Brits PvE Worlds Town Halls.

> 💡 **Note**
>
> Answers reflect the information provided by the development team at the time of the Town Hall. Some systems may change as development continues.

---

## Skill Tree

### Will the V1 Skill Tree return with the V2 UI?

No.
The V1 Skill Tree system will not return.
The development team replaced it because the old system was expensive on server performance and contained mechanics that did not fit the direction of Brits PvE Worlds.
The V2 Skill Tree is being developed as a custom system specifically for the server.

---

### Will more skills be added?

Yes.
The current Skill Tree launched with the minimum set of skills the development team was comfortable releasing.
Additional skills and specialized categories will be added over time.

---

### Why don't I have to unlock every skill leading up to a stronger skill?

This is intentional.
The V2 Skill Tree is designed to give players freedom to specialize rather than forcing them through a predefined path.
Players can invest directly into the skills that fit their playstyle.

---

### Will there be more Skill Tree categories?

Yes.
Additional specialized categories are planned.
These will work similarly to the Vehicles tree and will not necessarily contribute toward overall character level.

---

### Will XP gains be increased?

XP progression is being monitored and will be adjusted where necessary.
The developers can independently change XP gained from different activities and add additional XP sources.
Some current XP sources are also affected by bugs.

---

### How is XP divided between the three main trees?

The current intended total XP requirements are:

| Skill Tree | Total XP |
|---|---:|
| Gathercraft | 5,000,000 |
| Combat | 5,000,000 |
| Survivalcraft | 2,000,000 |

Survivalcraft requires less total XP because it currently has fewer available XP sources.

---

### What activities give XP to each tree?

**Gathercraft**

Activities involving base resources that naturally spawn in the world.

**Combat**

Activities involving shooting or fighting enemies.

**Survivalcraft**

Activities involving cultivation and survival, including farming, cooking, crafting-related activities, and skinning.

---

## Operations

### Can Operations be completed solo?

Operations are designed as group-based content.
The developers do not currently plan to create a dedicated solo version.
The system is intended to function similarly to group raids or MMO dungeon content.

---

### Why are Operations currently unavailable?

Operations have experienced issues with event servers not shutting down correctly.
Because the system dynamically creates servers for events, these shutdown issues can prevent new groups from receiving an available server.
The system is being worked on.

---

### Will Operations become harder?

Yes.
The first Operation is expected to receive balance and difficulty adjustments.
Additional Operations are also being developed.

---

### Will Operations get more mechanics?

Yes.
Future Operations are planned to include additional mechanics, modifiers, puzzles, and specialized content.

---

## Loot & Economy

### Were Legendary drop rates reduced in V2?

The development team stated that most Legendary drop rates were **not changed**.
The major V2 loot changes were primarily reductions to quantities of non-Legendary filler loot.
A small number of specific loot tables were adjusted.

---

### Are exact Legendary drop rates available?

No.
The development team does not disclose exact drop rates.
Loot tables can use weighted categories, making a single percentage difficult to represent accurately.

---

### Why does dungeon and raid loot feel worse in V2?

The development team considers this primarily a change in the gameplay and economy loop.
Players are now expected to:
1. Complete the content.
2. Recycle or sell unwanted loot.
3. Convert that loot into RP.
4. Use RP to purchase resources or items needed for future content.
The goal is to make the economy less dependent on receiving large quantities of already-useful items directly from events.

---

### Will Bradley loot be improved?

Bradley loot and difficulty are being reconsidered.
The current Bradley system is considered too easy for its rewards.
The planned Road Bradley system will provide a more challenging encounter and allow for better reward balancing.

---

### Will Deep Sea loot be changed?

The development team stated that Deep Sea Cargo loot quantities were changed, but the difficulty of the event itself was not changed.
Deep Sea timer and shutdown issues are also being investigated.

---

## Legendaries

### Will Legendary items be upgradeable?

Yes.
Upgradeable Legendaries are planned.
The system will not simply allow players to trade huge quantities of existing Legendaries for a better Legendary.

---

### Will there be new Legendary equipment?

Yes.
The development team plans to add more:
- Weapons
- Tools
- Armor
- Specialized gear
- Attachments
- Potential Legendary ammunition
The general direction is toward more specialized equipment rather than simply making every new weapon stronger.

---

### Will Legendary items have infinite durability?

No.
Infinite durability is not intended to remain.
However, the developers acknowledged that Legendary items currently lose durability too quickly and are working on ways to make the system less frustrating.

---

### Will Legendary durability be improved?

Yes.
Legendary items currently use the same durability-loss behavior as normal items.
The developers plan to reduce durability loss for Legendaries and introduce additional systems to help manage durability.

---

### Will Legendary items be skinable?

No, not currently.
The development team is concerned that changing Legendary skins could make it difficult for players to verify what they are buying during trades.

---

## Skill Commands & UI

### Will commands such as `/setgenes` and `/locatenodes` return?

Yes.
The development team confirmed that useful Skill Tree abilities will also be available through commands where appropriate.
Commands and UI access are intended to coexist.

---

### Is the `/setgenes` cooldown bug being fixed?

Yes.
The cooldown not progressing while offline was confirmed as a bug.
A fix is planned.

---

### Will the Skill Tree UI be improved?

Yes.
The development team acknowledged that the current UI does not communicate enough information clearly.
Further UI and UX improvements are planned.

---

## Prestige & Progression

### When can players Prestige?

Prestige is intended to become available at the appropriate level once the current Prestige-related bugs are resolved.
At the time of the Town Hall, Prestige was temporarily unavailable because of issues with the Prestige system.

---

### Will Prestige rewards survive the XP wipe?

Yes.
Prestige perks are intended to remain for the duration of the XP progression cycle rather than being treated like normal short-term wipe progression.

---

### Will XP eventually wipe less frequently?

Possibly, but not yet.
The developers currently use the longer XP cycle as a checkpoint while V2 and its major systems continue to stabilize.
The possibility of extending the XP cycle further in the future has been discussed.

---

## Trading & Shops

### Will Hub trade windows be added?

Yes.
Public player trade windows are planned for the Hub.
They cannot be introduced during the current wipe and are planned for a future wipe.

---

### Can players customize their Hub shops?

Not currently.
The development team has discussed allowing more customization, but Hub shops must remain compatible with the Hub's safe-zone and building restrictions.

---

## Farming & Cooking

### Will there be more farming quests?

Probably.
A farming-related quest system has been discussed, but it is not currently confirmed as a completed feature.

---

### Is cooking part of Survivalcraft XP?

Yes.
Cooking and farming activities fall under Survivalcraft.
Survivalcraft also includes activities related to cultivating and maintaining your character and base.

---

### Will C4 crafting be instant?

The lack of instant C4 crafting was identified as a bug and is on the development team's fix list.

---

## Achievements

### Why don't some Wipe Achievements appear to give rewards?

Some achievements are checkpoints toward a larger achievement rather than individual rewards.
The current UI does not communicate this clearly.
The achievement UI is planned to be redesigned to make progression and rewards clearer.

---

### Will Wipe Achievement rewards become more useful?

The developers want Wipe Achievement rewards to provide meaningful character bonuses.
Potential rewards include XP-related bonuses and other permanent character improvements, although the exact system is still being developed.

---

## Quality of Life

### Will Air Drop cooldowns be removed?

No.
The current cooldown exists partly to prevent players from spamming Air Drops and creating excessive item entities and server load.
A better system for managing multiple Air Drops is being considered instead.

---

### Will there be a mob spawner system?

It has been discussed, but it is not confirmed.
The developers have considered player-controlled spawners for animals and NPCs as a possible late-game system.

---

### Will there be monthly wipes?

No.
The development team does not currently plan to move to monthly wipes.

---

### Will there be a Mining World in AU?

It is being considered.
One possibility discussed was converting an AU raid world into a Mining World.

---

## Community & Future Development

### Can players suggest new Skill Tree skills?

Yes.
The development team wants players to submit constructive suggestions for future Skill Tree abilities.
A dedicated suggestion thread for Skill Tree ideas was discussed.

---

### Does the development team actually use player feedback?

Yes.
The developers stated that player feedback is actively used alongside gameplay data when balancing systems.
The Skill Tree, XP progression, loot, events, and future content are all expected to evolve based on feedback and collected data.

---

### Is Brits PvE Worlds finished with V2?

No.
V2 is still actively being developed.
The development team described the current systems as an early foundation that will be expanded, balanced, and refined over time.
